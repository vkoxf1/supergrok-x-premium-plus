# SuperGrok vs X Premium Plus: Which Subscription Gives You More for AI, X Features, and Everyday Use?

If you are comparing **SuperGrok vs X Premium Plus**, the confusing part is that both subscriptions can give you access to advanced Grok features, but they are designed around different priorities.

**SuperGrok is an AI-first subscription.** It is built for people who mainly want Grok on the web, mobile apps, and related xAI products.

**X Premium+ is an X-first subscription.** It bundles higher Grok access with X features such as an ad-reduced or mostly ad-free experience, a blue checkmark after eligibility review, X Pro, Articles, Radar Search, and stronger reply prioritization.

The simple answer is:

- Choose **SuperGrok** if your main goal is using Grok for research, writing, file analysis, image generation, video generation, voice, or AI workflows.
- Choose **X Premium+** if you already use X heavily and also want premium platform features alongside Grok.
- Do not assume the two subscriptions are identical just because both mention SuperGrok access. The billing channel, included X features, plan limits, and account activation process can differ.

Below is a practical comparison of price, features, usage limits, account access, and which plan makes more sense for different users.

The catch is that Premium+ is not simply “SuperGrok for $10 more.” You are paying for an X membership with Grok included. If you do not use X’s premium tools, part of the subscription is paying for features that will sit untouched.

## Is SuperGrok included with X Premium+?

This is where the wording gets messy.

X’s current official documentation says Premium+ includes **SuperGrok**, higher limits on the latest Grok models, Grok Bot, image and video generation, and voice mode.

However, WildAI’s own help center currently tells customers that Grok and X Premium+ are handled as separate products on its platform. It instructs users to select the specific Grok product if they want Grok, rather than assuming that an X subscription order also covers a Grok subscription.

Those statements are not necessarily describing the same purchasing route. X’s documentation describes what X includes when a customer subscribes directly through an eligible X account. WildAI’s instructions describe how its own order flow processes X Premium+ and Grok subscriptions.

The practical rule is:

1. If buying directly through X, check the Premium+ feature description attached to your account.
2. If ordering through a reseller or subscription service, select the exact product you want.
3. After activation, open Grok and confirm the account shows the expected access level.
4. Do not pay for both SuperGrok and X Premium+ until you verify whether the same account already has the required Grok access.

This is especially important because a subscription can be active on the wrong X account, the wrong email address, or the wrong login method. A technically successful payment does not automatically mean the intended Grok account is upgraded.

## Feature differences that actually matter

### 1. Access outside X

SuperGrok is built around Grok’s own environment. xAI says Grok is available on the web and in iOS and Android apps, with conversations, settings, and subscriptions synced across those platforms.

X Premium+ starts with the X account and X platform. It can provide advanced Grok access, but the account connection matters. If you mainly want a dedicated AI workspace, SuperGrok presents a clearer setup.

### 2. X platform tools

Premium+ wins decisively here.

You get features that SuperGrok does not provide, including:

- X Pro for multiple timelines and advanced search
- Articles for long-form publishing
- Higher reply prioritization
- A mostly ad-free X experience
- Premium creator and publishing tools
- Eligibility for the blue checkmark after review

These features have no equivalent in a standalone SuperGrok subscription. If you publish, moderate, research, or manage an audience on X, they may be the main reason to choose Premium+.

### 3. Images and video

Both subscriptions currently expose image and video capabilities through Grok, but the exact usage limits can change by plan, region, product surface, and billing channel.

xAI lists image generation and video generation as part of SuperGrok. X lists Imagine for videos and images under Premium+. Neither product page provides a simple universal number that applies to every user and every generation mode.

That means claims such as “you get exactly three times more generations” should be treated cautiously unless the number appears in your own account or current checkout information. The safest comparison is that both plans support these features, while paid usage is governed by plan-specific limits that can change.

### 4. Ads and account visibility

SuperGrok does not change your X advertising experience.

Premium+ is the relevant plan if you want fewer interruptions while browsing X. X says Premium+ is ad-free across many areas, including major timelines, replies, and profiles, although sponsored content may still appear. The ad-free feature also depends on account eligibility and the blue-check review process.

If you rarely use X, this benefit has little financial value. If you use X continuously, it can be one of the more noticeable differences between the subscriptions.

### 5. Long-running AI tasks

Both plans now connect to Grok Bot in the current product documentation. xAI describes Grok Bot as an AI teammate that can work on tasks, sign into tools, and operate through a persistent cloud computer. X also lists Grok Bot under Premium+ features.

The feature is still subject to availability, usage limits, and account eligibility. For occasional chat, it should not determine the purchase. For users who want agents and background tasks, it is worth confirming that the feature is active on the exact account being upgraded.

## Final verdict

The answer to **SuperGrok vs X Premium Plus** depends on what you are actually buying.

Choose **SuperGrok** when Grok is the main product. It costs less on monthly billing, keeps the subscription focused on AI, and is the more straightforward choice for research, writing, file analysis, image generation, video generation, voice, and Grok Bot use.
